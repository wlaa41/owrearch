## Where must wiring systems be supported such that they will not be liable to premature collapse in the event of a fire?

**Answer**: Wiring systems must be supported throughout the installation.

![Author](/assets/blog/ow-read-blog-sticky-man.webp)

### Understanding Regulation 521.10.202

Regulation 521.10.202, found on page 140 and in Chapter 52 under the section titled "Selection and erection of wiring systems" of the BS 7671 standards, mandates that wiring systems must be supported in such a way that they will not be liable to premature collapse in the event of a fire. This regulation applies to the entire installation, meaning that all wiring systems, regardless of their location—whether in escape routes, areas with increased fire risk, or not—must be adequately supported to prevent premature collapse.

#### Importance of Adequate Support

1. **Ensuring Safety in Escape Routes**: Ensuring that wiring systems do not collapse prematurely during a fire is critical for maintaining clear and safe escape routes for occupants.
2. **Preventing Secondary Hazards**: Unsupported wiring systems can fall and create additional hazards, such as obstructing pathways or causing electrical shorts, which can exacerbate the fire situation.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q: Why is it important to support wiring systems to prevent premature collapse during a fire?

**A**: Supporting wiring systems to prevent premature collapse is crucial for maintaining the integrity of escape routes and ensuring the safety of occupants during a fire.

#### Q: What are CA2 locations?

**A**: CA2 locations refer to areas with increased fire risk, such as places with combustible materials or processes that can exacerbate fire conditions.

1. What other measures can be taken to enhance fire safety concerning wiring systems?
**A**: Measures such as using fire-resistant materials, implementing regular maintenance checks, and adhering to all relevant regulations can enhance fire safety in wiring systems.
